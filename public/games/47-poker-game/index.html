How to play

Five-card draw poker vs one AI. Both ante, get 5 cards, discard up to 3, draw once, then showdown.

  • Ante — both pay the ante into the pot (2× ante).
  • Discard — tap cards to mark (max 3), then Draw for replacements.
  • Showdown — best 5-card hand wins the pot. Ties split.
  • Ranks: RF > SF > Quads > Full House > Flush > Straight > Trips > Two Pair > Pair > High Card.
  • Desktop & mobile: tap cards and buttons. Easy includes discard hints.
